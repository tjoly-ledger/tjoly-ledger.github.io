nbgl_useCaseSpinner

A waiting page with no control, whose spinner turns on its own every 800 ms. Freezing the emulated time holds the page still long enough to capture, but not at a fixed phase of the glyph, so this is the one screen of the catalog that comes out differently from one run to the next.
